Moshi Fine-tuning Demo

Teaching a full-duplex speech model when to stay silent and when to speak again. Each conversation below was never seen in training; the same recording is played to the base model and to Moshi fine-tuned on 45 h of synthetic conversations. The silence comes from the model itself; there is no mute rule.

Transcripts: user and friend lines are the script; blue is what Moshi generated, red is Moshi talking during the requested silence, and gray italics are Moshi's scripted lines replayed before the part being tested.
